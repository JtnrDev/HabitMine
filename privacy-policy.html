Privacy Policy for HabitMine

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the HabitMine mobile application (“HabitMine”, the “App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information. It applies to the HabitMine Android application published on Google Play under the package name com.jtnrdev.dailyhabittracker.

Please read this policy carefully. By installing or using HabitMine, you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Developer: jtnrdev
Contact email: jtnrdev@gmail.com

1. Summary (plain language)

  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.
  • Everything you create in HabitMine — your habits, categories, daily progress, streaks, reminders, and settings — is stored only on your device.
  • We do not use any advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
  • We have no servers and no user accounts. You do not sign in to use HabitMine.
  • The only third parties involved are Google Play services (for optional in-app purchases and the optional “Rate us” prompt), which operate under Google’s own privacy policy.

The sections below describe this in full detail.

2. Information we do and do not collect

2.1 Information you create in the App (“App Content”)

When you use HabitMine, you may create and store content such as:

  • Habit definitions (name, icon, color, type, target/unit, frequency, reminder time, sort order);
  • Categories you create or edit;
  • Daily completion logs and progress amounts, from which streaks and statistics are derived;
  • App settings (theme preference, Insights look-back period).

This App Content is stored locally on your device using Android’s on-device storage (a private SQLite/Room database and private app preferences). It is not transmitted to us and is not accessible to us. We never see it.

2.2 Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect, request, or process any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or account identifiers;
  • Precise or approximate location;
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, files, camera, or microphone data;
  • Advertising identifiers (we do not use an advertising ID);
  • Device identifiers, usage analytics, behavioral data, or diagnostics;
  • Health or medical data in any clinical sense (habit tracking is for personal self-improvement only and is not a medical or health service).

HabitMine contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs.

3. Third-party services

HabitMine integrates a small number of Google-provided services. We do not control how Google processes data through these services; their handling is governed by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

3.1 Google Play Billing (optional in-app purchase)

HabitMine offers a single optional one-time purchase (“Full Version”). If you choose to buy it, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google collects and processes your payment information; we never receive, see, or store your payment details or billing address. HabitMine only stores a local on/off flag indicating whether the Full Version is unlocked, which it re-verifies against Google Play. See the Google Play Terms of Service and Google’s privacy policy for how Google handles purchase data.

3.2 Google Play In-App Review (optional)

HabitMine uses the Google Play In-App Review feature to let you optionally rate the App. This feature is operated by Google; its outcome is not reported back to us, and we do not learn whether or how you rated the App.

3.3 Google Play Services / network access

The in-app purchase and review features require network access and therefore the INTERNET permission, which is included by the Google Play libraries. HabitMine itself makes no other network connections and sends no data of its own over the network. Any network communication by the Google libraries uses industry-standard encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS).

3.4 Email feedback (optional, user-initiated)

If you tap “Contact us” in Settings, the App opens your own email app with a blank message addressed to our support address (jtnrdev@gmail.com). No message is sent unless you write and send it. If you choose to email us, we will receive the email address and content you send, and we will use it only to respond to your request. We do not add you to any mailing list.

4. Data storage, backups, and export

4.1 On-device storage

All App Content is stored in HabitMine’s private storage area on your device, accessible only to the App.

4.2 Android Auto Backup

HabitMine allows Android’s built-in Auto Backup feature, which may back up the App’s data to your own Google account / Google Drive backup, subject to your device’s backup settings. This backup is controlled by Android and your Google account — not by us — and we have no access to it. You can disable Android backups in your device’s system settings.

4.3 Manual backup and restore (you control the file)

HabitMine lets you export your data to a JSON backup file and restore it, using the Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework). You choose where the backup file is saved (for example, local storage or a cloud drive you select). The file stays under your control; we never receive it. If you store a backup in a third-party location, that location’s privacy policy applies to it.

5. Permissions we request and why

PermissionWhy HabitMine uses it
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show habit reminder notifications and the live duration-timer notification. You can grant or deny this; the App works without it (timers run silently).
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE To keep a habit duration timer (stopwatch) running and stoppable from its notification while the App is in the background or closed.
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Included by Google Play Billing / Review libraries for the optional purchase and rating features.
WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Included by the Android WorkManager library used for scheduling reminders.

HabitMine does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, or device files.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because all App Content lives on your device:

  • Uninstalling HabitMine removes all App Content stored by the App on your device.
  • You can delete individual habits, categories, or their history at any time from within the App.
  • If you have enabled Android Auto Backup, a copy may remain in your Google account backup until it expires or you delete it through your Google account / device backup settings.
  • Any JSON backup files you exported are under your control; delete them yourself from wherever you saved them.

Since we do not hold any of your personal data on our side, there is no server-side data for us to delete. If you email us for support, you may ask us to delete the correspondence you sent, and we will do so.

7. Children’s privacy

HabitMine is a general-audience productivity app. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because we collect no personal information at all, we do not knowingly process children’s data. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through the optional email feature, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), including rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal data, and to object to or restrict processing.

Because HabitMine does not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is generally no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete. You remain in full control of your on-device content and can delete it at any time as described in Section 6. For any question about your rights, contact us at jtnrdev@gmail.com.

  • We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under CCPA/CPRA.
  • For purchases and ratings, Google is the relevant data controller/processor; please refer to Google’s privacy policy for the rights available to you in relation to data Google processes.

9. Data security

Your App Content is stored in HabitMine’s private, sandboxed storage, which other apps cannot read under normal Android security. Communication performed by the Google Play libraries uses encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS). No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security; however, because we do not transmit or store your personal data off your device, the data we could be responsible for is minimal.

10. International users and export

HabitMine is distributed through Google Play. The App uses only standard encryption provided by the Android operating system and Google’s libraries (such as HTTPS/TLS) and contains no proprietary cryptography. The App is available subject to Google Play’s country availability and applicable export laws.
